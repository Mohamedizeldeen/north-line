## Positioning

- **EN:** The complete system for Omani fashion stores — online store, POS, and one unified inventory.

## Ideal customer

A fashion or abaya boutique in Muscat with 1–3 branches that also sells through
Instagram DMs. **The decision maker is the owner — usually a woman, non-technical,
and she reads and buys in Arabic.** She is not a developer and does not care what
the software is built with.

## The problem we solve

1. **Double inventory** — the same piece is sold in-store *and* online because
   the two stocks are separate. "بعنا نفس العباية مرتين"
2. **Orders lost in Instagram DMs** — order, size and address arrive in three
   separate messages. "الطلبات تضيع بين رسائل الإنستقرام"
3. **No reporting** — no idea what sells and what sits. "ما أعرف كم قطعة باقية"

## Product stack (all already built, not vapourware)

| Product | What it does for her |
| --- | --- |
| Online store | Arabic, mobile-first, customers order and pay themselves |
| AI virtual try-on | She sees the piece on herself before ordering — fewer returns |
| POS | Fast touch till for the shop; deducts stock on sale |
| Unified inventory | One stock across every branch and the online store |
| Accounting | Sales, expenses, profit, VAT-return ready |
| Email marketing | Bring past customers back for new collections |

Payments: **Amwal Pay** — an Omani gateway licensed by the Central Bank of Oman.
Gateway approval needs a CR and VAT certificate and takes 3–10 business days.

What only we do: **POS + online store + one unified inventory + virtual try-on**,
sold and supported locally in Oman.

## Hard content constraints

- **Arabic is the primary language.** English is secondary.
- **Zero technical jargon.** Never write Laravel, API, framework, stack, cloud,
